# Clipper Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 24, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 24, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Clipper (“we,” “our,” or “the application”) is a video clipping and publishing tool designed to help users prepare short-form videos and, when configured, publish content to supported social media platforms, including Instagram.

This Privacy Policy explains how Clipper may collect, use, store, and disclose information when you use the application and its integrations with Meta platforms.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you enable, Clipper may process the following information:

- **Account and authorization information:** Meta account identifiers, access permissions, and authentication tokens required to connect your account and use authorized features.
- **Instagram account information:** Basic account identifiers and profile information made available through the permissions you authorize.
- **Video and publishing information:** Videos you select for processing or publishing, captions, titles, thumbnails, publishing status, and related metadata.
- **Technical information:** Application logs, error messages, and diagnostic information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the service.

Clipper does not intentionally request your Meta password. Authentication is handled through Meta's authorization systems.

The specific information processed depends on your configuration, the permissions you grant, and the features you use.

## 3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- Connect your authorized social media account to Clipper.
- Process videos and prepare short-form content.
- Publish content to Instagram when you initiate or configure that action.
- Display publishing status and help troubleshoot failed operations.
- Maintain application security, reliability, and performance.
- Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use authorized account information for purposes unrelated to operating, securing, or improving the application's intended functionality.

## 4. How Information Is Stored

Clipper may process information on the computer or server where it is installed. Depending on your setup, video files, configuration settings, logs, publishing records, and authentication tokens may be stored locally or by services you choose to use.

You are responsible for securing your computer, server, configuration files, backups, and any stored credentials.

You should not share access tokens, application secrets, or other authentication credentials. Access tokens should be protected and deleted when they are no longer needed.

## 5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

Clipper may exchange information with third-party services when necessary to provide enabled features.

**Meta and Instagram:** When you connect an account or publish content, information is sent to Meta as necessary to authenticate your account and perform the actions you authorize. Meta's handling of information is also governed by its own terms and privacy policies.

Clipper does not sell personal information. We do not disclose personal information to unrelated third parties for their own advertising purposes. Information may be disclosed when necessary to provide a requested service, protect security, or comply with applicable law.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Video files, logs, publishing records, and account-related information may remain on your local computer or server until you delete them or configure the application to remove them. You should review and remove stored files and credentials that you no longer need.
